How it works

One conversation, seven quiet steps

Every Adhyay session follows the same gentle arc — from naming a feeling to taking one small, real action.

1

Choose your emotional state

Start with what's true right now — overwhelmed, overthinking, doubting yourself, confused, or heavy.

2

Answer a guided follow-up question

Adhyay asks what's actually making it feel that way — adapting each question to your last answer.

3

Receive a personalised session

Based on what you shared, we recommend one specific session with a clear focus and duration.

4

Breathe, first

A slow, animated breathing circle brings your body down before we ask your mind to reflect.

5

Reflect, one prompt at a time

Short, structured prompts and one hands-on exercise — never one overwhelming blank page.

6

Receive your insight

A compassionate, specific reflection of the pattern in what you wrote.

7

Take one small action

A single achievable step for today — nothing more.

Frequently asked

Common questions

What is Adhyay Foundation? +

Adhyay Foundation is a guided thinking and mental clarity platform designed to help people understand their emotions, organize their thoughts, and gain clarity through interactive reflection sessions.

Is Adhyay a meditation app? +

No. Unlike traditional meditation apps, Adhyay focuses on guided thinking, reflection, emotional understanding, and practical clarity instead of only relaxation.

How does Adhyay work? +

You first select how you're feeling through "What's Your Adhyay Today?" — the platform then recommends a suitable session based on your emotional state.

Does Adhyay replace therapy? +

No. Adhyay is a self-guided clarity platform and not a replacement for professional medical or mental health treatment.

Who can use Adhyay? +

Anyone can use Adhyay, especially students and young people who want emotional clarity, calmness, and a better understanding of their thoughts.

What is the meaning of "Adhyay"? +

"Adhyay" means "chapter." It represents the different emotional chapters in a person's life and the journey toward clarity and self-understanding.
